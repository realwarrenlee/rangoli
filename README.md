# A welcome that grows

A Vite web experience for Common Ground Makeathon, with seven connected scroll chapters exploring Vijayalakshmi Mohan's practice of rangoli and the welcome it creates.

Use native scrolling; no clicks are required in the illustrated story. The operating system's reduced-motion preference automatically selects a complete illustrated reading version. The optional [Roots.sg](https://roots.sg/) film at the end loads when you click its play button.

## Local development

Run these commands from `rangoli`:

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the URL printed in the terminal.

## Story

1. A child begins making rangoli beside her mother.
2. Doorstep patterns welcome guests and carry hopes for the home.
3. Familiar shapes and new materials make rangoli accessible to more people.
4. A community makes a rangoli together, then shares the sweets and snacks used in the pattern.
5. Newspaper offers another way to participate when people cannot gather during the pandemic.
6. One person learns, then passes the practice on to others.
7. Another person begins a design of their own, continuing a welcome that grows.

## Profile

[Stewards of Intangible Cultural Heritage 2024 - Vijayalakshmi Mohan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S7i09mka5ec) · [Roots Sg](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCqj-dZ58ntJFxij7QUB94Uw)

Mrs Vijayalakshmi Mohan has been a dedicated practitioner of rangoli with over 60 years of experience in the craft. She has created more than 8,000 rangolis in Singapore, and over 20,000 rangolis globally. Notably, she is the current Guinness World Record holder for the largest rangoli pattern created. As the founder of Singa Rangoli, she actively experiments with new materials and contemporary designs for her rangoli works. Beyond her own practice, Mrs Vijaya Mohan promotes rangoli through art classes and workshops, and trains a team of practitioners who conduct regular workshops for schools and community groups. As a trained arts therapist, she works with welfare organisations to conduct rangoli programmes for underserved communities.
